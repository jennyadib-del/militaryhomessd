5 mistakes first-time VA buyers make in San Diego

By Jenny Adib · September 9, 2026

Buying your first home with a VA loan is exciting. Here are the five mistakes I see most often, and how to avoid them.

1. Picking a lender only for the lowest advertised rate

A lender who doesn't close VA loans often can cause delays that cost you the house. Compare rates, but also ask how many VA loans they close and how fast they order appraisals.

2. Shopping by approval amount instead of payment

Just because you're approved for a price doesn't mean the payment fits your life. Factor in property taxes, insurance, HOA, utilities, and what happens if your BAH changes after your next PCS.

3. Ignoring HOA dues and Mello-Roos

Many newer San Diego communities, especially in Chula Vista and other planned areas, have HOA dues and Mello-Roos special taxes that can add meaningfully to your monthly payment. Always ask before you write an offer.

4. Skipping the inspection because "the VA appraisal checks it"

A VA appraisal is not a home inspection. It confirms value and minimum property requirements. A full home inspection protects you from costly surprises.

5. Not thinking about the next PCS

Ask yourself: if I get orders in three years, could I rent this home for close to my payment? Homes near bases with solid layouts tend to be easier to rent and resell.

Avoid all five with a plan. Read my VA loan buyer guide or learn about VA loan benefits in California.
