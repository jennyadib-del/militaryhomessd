Sell Your Home During a PCS in San Diego

Orders came in and the clock is ticking. Find out what your home is worth, compare selling vs. renting, and sell on your timeline, even after you've left San Diego.

Selling a home during a PCS is a different job than a typical sale. Your deadline is fixed, you may be moving across the country (or the world), and the decision to sell or rent affects your finances for years.

I'm a Navy Veteran and San Diego military realtor. I've helped military homeowners sell quickly, sell for top dollar, and sell remotely, and I've helped others decide that keeping the home as a rental was the smarter move.

Sell, rent, or hold? Know your numbers first

Your free home value report includes a side-by-side comparison:

  • Selling: estimated sale price, costs of sale, and your estimated net proceeds.
  • Renting: estimated monthly rent, mortgage, property management and maintenance, and monthly cash flow.
  • Your next purchase: how either choice affects your VA entitlement for buying at your next duty station.

How I sell homes for PCSing families

A plan built around your report date

We work backward from your PCS date: prep, photos, list date, offer review and closing, so you're never left scrambling.

Prep and marketing that gets results

Professional photography, video, targeted online marketing and exposure to military buyers coming to San Diego on orders.

Sell from anywhere

Already gone? I can coordinate showings, vendors and repairs while you're away, with digital signing and remote or mobile notary options for closing. If needed, a power of attorney can help. Ask your legal office about the right type for your situation.

Market your low-rate VA loan

If your VA loan has a low interest rate, an assumption could make your home more attractive to buyers. I'll walk you through the pros and cons for your entitlement.

Tax questions to ask before selling

Homeowners who have owned and lived in their home for 2 of the last 5 years may be able to exclude a large portion of the gain from capital gains tax. Service members on qualified official extended duty may be able to suspend that 5-year period for up to 10 years, which can help if you've rented the home out after a PCS. Talk with a tax professional about your specific situation.

Selling during a PCS: FAQs

How fast can I sell my home in San Diego before a PCS?
A well-priced, well-prepared San Diego home can often go under contract within a few weeks, followed by a typical 30 to 45 day escrow. Starting 60 to 90 days before your report date gives you the most options.
Can I sell my house after I've already moved?
Yes. I manage showings, vendors and paperwork locally, and you sign documents electronically or with a remote or mobile notary.
Should I sell or rent my San Diego home when I PCS?
It depends on your cash flow, equity, plans to return, tax situation and whether you need your full VA entitlement for your next home. Your free home value report includes both scenarios side by side.
