FAQ

Questions military buyers ask me most

Why work with a military realtor in San Diego?
A military realtor understands PCS timelines, BAH, VA loan rules and the neighborhoods around each base. That means fewer surprises, offers structured to compete, and a plan that accounts for your next set of orders, whether that's renting the home out or selling it.
Can I buy a home in San Diego before I arrive on PCS orders?
Yes. Many of my clients buy remotely using live video tours, digital signatures and mobile or remote notary services, then move straight into their home when they arrive.
Do sellers in San Diego accept VA offers?
Yes. A well-prepared VA offer with a strong pre-approval, a responsive lender and clean terms competes well. Part of my job is presenting your offer so the listing agent feels confident in it.
Does it cost anything to talk with you?
No. Consultations are free, and you'll leave with a clear plan whether or not we work together.
