Military Base Housing

Homes Near San Diego Military Bases

Where you live shapes your commute, your kids' schools, and your rental value when the next orders arrive. Here's a base-by-base look at where military families buy in San Diego County.

Base by base

Neighborhoods near each installation

Commute times vary a lot with traffic and time of day. I'll map your real drive at your report time before you buy.

Naval Base San Diego (32nd Street)

Chula Vista, Eastlake, Otay Ranch, Bonita, National City, Lemon Grove, La Mesa, Spring Valley and South Park.

NAS North Island & NAB Coronado

Coronado, Imperial Beach, Chula Vista and the South Bay, plus downtown-adjacent neighborhoods via the bridge.

Naval Base Point Loma

Point Loma, Ocean Beach, Loma Portal, Clairemont, Bay Park and Mission Hills.

MCAS Miramar

Mira Mesa, Scripps Ranch, Tierrasanta, Rancho Peñasquitos, Santee, Poway and Clairemont.

MCB Camp Pendleton

Oceanside, Vista, Fallbrook, Carlsbad, San Marcos and, to the north, San Clemente.

Naval Medical Center San Diego & MCRD

North Park, South Park, Golden Hill, Kensington, Normal Heights, Point Loma and Clairemont.

How to choose a neighborhood near your base

  • Commute: San Diego traffic is very directional. Living "against" the commute can save real time each day.
  • Schools: Look up school boundaries for any address you're considering, and visit if you can.
  • Rentability: If you might keep the home as a rental after your next PCS, being close to a base helps with future military tenants.
  • Budget vs. BAH: Inland areas like Santee, Spring Valley and Oceanside often stretch a VA budget further than coastal neighborhoods.
  • HOA and Mello-Roos: Many newer communities have HOA dues and Mello-Roos special taxes that affect your monthly payment.

Want deeper detail on the areas around the Navy's largest base? Read my guide to the best places to live near Naval Base San Diego, or start a military home search.

What is the most affordable area near San Diego military bases?
Inland and South Bay communities such as Spring Valley, Lemon Grove, National City, Santee and parts of Oceanside and Vista are often more budget-friendly than coastal areas, though prices vary by home and street.
Is it better to live on base or off base in San Diego?
On-base housing offers convenience and a built-in community, but waitlists can be long. Living off base with a VA loan lets you build equity and potentially keep the home as a rental after you PCS.
