Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jenny Adib Enterprise LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), a real estate salesperson (CA DRE #02208618) affiliated with Real Broker, LLC (CA DRE #02022092), collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or contact us.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, property address, home search preferences, PCS details and any messages you submit through our forms.
  • Information collected automatically: device and browser information, pages visited, referring website and similar analytics data collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide real estate services you request
  • To send home searches, market information, home valuations and resources you request
  • To contact you by phone, email or text message when you have given consent
  • To operate, maintain and improve this website
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

SMS / text messaging

If you opt in to receive text messages, we use your mobile number only to communicate with you about real estate services. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or personal information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. See our Terms & Conditions for full SMS terms.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers that help us operate our business (such as our CRM, email and website hosting providers), who are permitted to use it only to provide services to us. This sharing never includes mobile opt-in data or consent for those providers' own marketing.
  • With our brokerage, Real Broker, LLC, as required for compliance and recordkeeping
  • With parties involved in your real estate transaction (such as lenders or escrow) at your direction
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of others

Your choices

  • Email: click "unsubscribe" in any email.
  • Text: reply STOP to any message.
  • Access or deletion: email jennyadib@militaryhomessd.com to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information.

California privacy rights

California residents may have rights under California law to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us using the information below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use this website. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Data security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.
