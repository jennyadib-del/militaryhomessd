PCS Relocation Guide

PCS to San Diego, made simple

Orders to San Diego? Welcome to America's Finest City. Here's how to plan your move, choose a neighborhood, and decide whether to buy or rent, plus a free guide to keep it all organized.

A PCS to San Diego is exciting, and it comes with a lot to figure out fast: housing, schools, commutes, and how far your BAH will go in one of the country's priciest markets.

I'm a Navy Veteran and San Diego military realtor, and I help families PCS here every season. Use this guide as your starting point, then grab the free downloadable PCS guide for the full checklist.

Your San Diego PCS timeline

WhenWhat to do
As soon as you have ordersContact your installation's housing office, request your sponsor, look up your BAH rate, and talk to a VA lender about pre-approval.
60–90 days outNarrow down neighborhoods by base and commute. Start virtual tours. Decide whether to buy now or rent first.
30–60 days outSchedule your household goods shipment. Make offers if buying. Line up school enrollment and childcare.
ArrivalClose and move in, or settle into temporary lodging (TLA) while you finish your search.

Buy or rent when you PCS to San Diego?

There's no one right answer. It depends on how long you expect to be here, your BAH, and whether you'd keep the home as a rental after your next set of orders. Many military families buy with a VA loan, live in the home for their tour, then rent it out and build equity long-term.

  • Buying may make sense if you'll be here 3+ years, want stability for kids, or plan to keep a rental in San Diego.
  • Renting first may make sense on a short tour, if you're unsure which area fits, or if your budget needs more time.

I'll run both scenarios with you, at no cost.

San Diego military installations at a glance

San Diego County is home to Naval Base San Diego, Naval Air Station North Island, Naval Amphibious Base Coronado, Naval Base Point Loma, MCAS Miramar, Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton, MCRD San Diego and Naval Medical Center San Diego. See neighborhoods and commutes for each in my homes near San Diego military bases guide.

Understanding BAH in San Diego

Basic Allowance for Housing is set by ZIP code of your duty station, pay grade and dependency status, and it's updated every January. Look up your exact rate with the official DoD BAH calculator at travel.dod.mil. I'll help you see how your BAH compares to a monthly mortgage payment in the neighborhoods you're considering.

Buying remotely before you arrive

You don't have to be here to buy here. I regularly help clients purchase with live FaceTime tours, detailed video walk-throughs, digital document signing and remote notary options, so you can arrive with keys in hand.

PCS to San Diego FAQs

How far in advance should I start looking for a home in San Diego?
Ideally 60 to 90 days before your report date. That gives you time for pre-approval, virtual tours and a typical 30 to 45 day escrow.
Is on-base housing available in San Diego?
Military family housing is available but waitlists can be long depending on rank, bedroom need and community. Contact your installation's housing office as soon as you have orders, and keep an off-base plan ready.
What are the best neighborhoods for military families in San Diego?
It depends on your base. Popular choices include Chula Vista and Bonita near 32nd Street, Coronado and Imperial Beach near North Island, Santee and Scripps Ranch near Miramar, and Oceanside near Camp Pendleton. See my neighborhood guide.
