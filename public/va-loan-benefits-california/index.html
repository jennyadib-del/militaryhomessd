VA Loan Education

VA Loan Benefits in California

Plain-English answers about your VA home loan benefit, plus California-specific programs that can save veterans money.

The core VA loan benefits

  • No down payment for eligible borrowers with full entitlement.
  • No monthly PMI, unlike most low-down-payment conventional loans.
  • Competitive rates because the VA guarantees part of the loan.
  • Limits on closing costs charged to veterans, and seller concessions allowed.
  • No prepayment penalty. Pay it off early any time.
  • Reusable benefit: use it again after selling, or keep a VA-financed home as a rental and buy again with remaining entitlement.

Understanding entitlement

Entitlement is the amount the VA guarantees on your loan. Borrowers with full entitlement have no VA loan limit, so lenders can approve $0 down at higher price points based on income and credit. That matters in San Diego, where home prices are high. If you have an active VA loan, your remaining entitlement and the county loan limit affect how much you can borrow without a down payment.

The VA funding fee

Most VA borrowers pay a one-time funding fee, which can be rolled into the loan. The fee depends on your down payment and whether it's your first VA loan. Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability, and certain others, are exempt. Your lender will confirm your exact fee and exemption status.

Seller concessions

VA rules allow sellers to pay a buyer's normal closing costs, plus concessions (such as prepaid taxes and insurance, or funding fee) up to 4% of the home's value. In the right market, I negotiate these credits to reduce your cash to close.

California veteran benefits to know

CalVet Home Loans

California's Department of Veterans Affairs offers the CalVet Home Loan program, which can be used alone or combined with VA guarantee features. Ask your lender whether it fits your situation.

Disabled Veterans' Property Tax Exemption

California offers a property tax exemption for qualifying veterans with a 100% disability rating (or paid at the 100% rate for unemployability), and in some cases their surviving spouses. In San Diego County, you apply through the County Assessor.

Veterans' Exemption

A smaller veterans' exemption may also be available to eligible veterans who meet certain property ownership limits. The County Assessor can confirm eligibility.

Are VA loans available in all of California?
Yes. VA loans are a federal benefit and can be used anywhere in California for a primary residence, including single-family homes, VA-approved condos and 2–4 unit properties where you live in one unit.
Can I buy a duplex or fourplex with a VA loan in San Diego?
Yes. You can buy a 2–4 unit property with a VA loan if you live in one of the units as your primary residence. Rental income from the other units may help you qualify.
Who is exempt from the VA funding fee?
Generally, veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability, certain surviving spouses, and service members who've received a Purple Heart are exempt. Your lender confirms exemption using your Certificate of Eligibility.

VA, CalVet and property tax program rules change and are determined by the administering agencies. Confirm details with your lender, the California Department of Veterans Affairs or the San Diego County Assessor. This is not lending or tax advice.
