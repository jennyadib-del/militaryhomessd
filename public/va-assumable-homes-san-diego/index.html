VA Loan Assumptions

VA Assumable Homes in San Diego

Some San Diego homes come with a VA loan you can take over, often at an interest rate far below today's. Here's how VA loan assumptions work and how to find them.

A VA loan assumption lets a buyer take over the seller's existing VA mortgage, including its interest rate, remaining balance and remaining term.

Many homeowners locked in VA loans when rates were much lower than they are now. When those homes sell, an assumption can mean a dramatically lower monthly payment for the buyer. As a San Diego military realtor, I track VA assumable homes and help buyers and sellers through the process.

How a VA loan assumption works

  1. Find an assumable listing. Not every listing advertises it, so I dig through listings and talk to agents to find them.
  2. Cover the equity gap. You pay the difference between the purchase price and the seller's remaining loan balance, usually in cash or, in some cases, with a second loan.
  3. Qualify with the loan servicer. The servicer reviews your credit and income, much like a regular loan approval.
  4. Pay the assumption funding fee. The VA funding fee on an assumption is 0.5% of the loan balance, unless you're exempt.
  5. Close. Assumptions often take longer than a typical purchase, so plan for a longer escrow.

Who can assume a VA loan?

Both veterans and non-veterans can assume a VA loan if they qualify with the servicer. The key difference is entitlement:

  • If the buyer is an eligible veteran and substitutes their own entitlement, the seller's entitlement is restored to use on their next home.
  • If the buyer doesn't substitute entitlement, the seller's entitlement stays tied to that loan until it's paid off. Sellers need to understand this before agreeing to an assumption.
Example: A home sells for $850,000 and the seller's VA loan balance is $600,000. The buyer assumes the $600,000 loan at the seller's rate and covers the $250,000 equity gap. Your actual numbers will vary, so I'll run them for any home you're considering.

Selling a home with an assumable VA loan

If you have a low-rate VA loan, it can be a major selling point that makes your home stand out. I'll help you market the assumption, screen buyers, and protect your entitlement. Learn more about selling your home during a PCS.

VA assumable home FAQs

Do I have to be a veteran to assume a VA loan?
No. Non-veterans can assume a VA loan if they qualify with the servicer. However, the seller's VA entitlement stays with that loan unless a veteran buyer substitutes their own entitlement.
How long does a VA loan assumption take?
Timelines vary by servicer, and assumptions commonly take longer than a standard purchase. Plan for a longer escrow and build that into your PCS timeline.
How do I find VA assumable homes in San Diego?
Assumable loans aren't always advertised. Request a search and I'll flag homes with assumable VA loans as they come up.

Assumption approval, fees and timelines are set by the loan servicer and the VA. This information is educational and not lending advice.
