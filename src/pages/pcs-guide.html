1. PCS relocation checklist

As soon as you have orders

  • Request your sponsor and contact your new installation's housing office
  • Look up your BAH rate on the official DoD BAH calculator
  • Schedule your household goods move or PPM through your transportation office
  • Talk with a VA lender and get pre-approved if you might buy
  • Request school records and check enrollment requirements
  • Ask your legal office about a power of attorney if you may need one

60–90 days out

  • Choose 2–3 target neighborhoods by base and commute
  • Start live video tours with your realtor
  • Decide: buy now, rent first, or on-base housing
  • If you own a home at your current station, decide whether to sell or rent it

30–60 days out

  • Make offers and open escrow (if buying)
  • Book temporary lodging (TLA/TLE) as a backup
  • Set up utilities: SDG&E (gas & electric), water and internet
  • Plan your vehicle registration and California driver's license steps

Arrival week

  • Check in with your command and the installation housing office
  • Final walk-through and closing, or move into temporary lodging
  • Confirm household goods delivery date
