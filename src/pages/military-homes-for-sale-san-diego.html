What VA buyers should look for in a San Diego listing

VA appraisals check Minimum Property Requirements. When reviewing military homes for sale in San Diego, watch for:

  • Peeling or chipping paint on homes built before 1978
  • Roof condition and remaining life
  • Working heat, water heater and safe electrical
  • Handrails on stairs, and safe access to the home
  • Unpermitted additions or garage conversions

None of these are automatic deal-breakers. Many can be negotiated as seller repairs before closing. I'll flag them early so there are no surprises.
