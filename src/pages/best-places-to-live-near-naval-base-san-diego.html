The "best" place to live near Naval Base San Diego depends on your budget, your commute tolerance and your family's lifestyle. As a Navy Veteran, here's my honest take on the neighborhoods I show most often.

Chula Vista (Eastlake, Otay Ranch, Rolling Hills Ranch)

Best for: families who want newer homes, parks and planned communities.

Chula Vista is one of the most popular choices for Navy families. Eastlake and Otay Ranch offer newer construction, community pools, and shopping close by. Watch for HOA dues and Mello-Roos in newer communities when comparing payments.

Bonita

Best for: more space, larger lots and a quieter, semi-rural feel.

Bonita is tucked between Chula Vista and National City, with larger lots, horse properties and established neighborhoods, while staying a short drive to 32nd Street.

National City

Best for: the shortest commute and more affordable price points.

National City borders the base, so commutes can be very short. It's a practical choice for buyers focused on value and convenience, and homes here can rent well to military tenants later.

La Mesa & Lemon Grove

Best for: charming older homes, walkable village areas and a central location.

La Mesa offers a walkable village, established neighborhoods and access to the freeways that connect to both 32nd Street and other bases. Lemon Grove is a neighboring option with friendlier price points.

Coronado & Imperial Beach

Best for: beach lifestyle, especially for dual-base families with North Island or NAB Coronado.

Coronado is iconic and pricey, with a strong military community. Imperial Beach offers a laid-back beach town feel at more approachable prices.

Spring Valley

Best for: stretching a VA budget with more square footage.

Spring Valley often delivers more house and yard for the money, with reasonable access to the base via the 94 and 125.
