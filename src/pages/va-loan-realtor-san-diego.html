Why VA buyers choose a VA loan realtor

  • $0 down payment for eligible buyers with full entitlement, with no VA loan limit in that case.
  • No private mortgage insurance (PMI), which can save hundreds per month compared with a low-down conventional loan.
  • Competitive interest rates and flexible credit guidelines set by VA-approved lenders.
  • Seller concessions: VA rules allow sellers to contribute toward your closing costs and certain other expenses.
  • Funding fee exemption for many veterans receiving VA disability compensation.

VA loan FAQs

Can I use my VA loan more than once?
Yes. The VA benefit is reusable. You may be able to restore entitlement after selling a home, or use remaining entitlement to buy again while keeping a previous home as a rental.
Is there a VA loan limit in San Diego?
Borrowers with full entitlement don't have a VA loan limit, though lenders still set limits based on income and credit. If you have a VA loan already in use, the county conforming loan limit can affect how much you can borrow with $0 down.
Do VA loans take longer to close?
Not necessarily. With an experienced lender, VA loans often close in a timeline similar to conventional loans. Appraisal scheduling is the main variable, and I plan around it.
Can a spouse use a VA loan?
Certain surviving spouses are eligible for VA home loan benefits. A spouse can also be a co-borrower with an eligible service member. A VA lender can confirm eligibility for your situation.

VA loan eligibility, entitlement, funding fees and terms are determined by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs and your lender. This page is educational and is not lending advice.
