About Jenny

Meet Jenny Adib

Hi, I'm Jenny! And if there's one thing you should know about me, it's that I value relationships over transactions, each and every single time.

Before becoming a Realtor, I proudly served eight years in the U.S. Navy. So trust me when I say I understand the stress of PCS orders, tight timelines, moving your family, and making some of the biggest financial decisions of your life, sometimes all at once.

I've been in your shoes, PCSing to San Diego as a dual-military family with a toddler and a dog, and that's exactly why I'm so passionate about helping military families and Veterans navigate the homebuying and selling process.

I approach every move like a mission: a clear plan, consistent communication, and no unnecessary surprises.

Whether you're an E-4 purchasing your first home using your VA loan, an officer PCSing to San Diego with a family of five, or a Veteran getting ready to sell your home, you'll receive the same level of care, dedication, and advocacy.

As a first-generation Latina, Navy Veteran, and mom, I know how important it is to have someone in your corner who genuinely understands your journey.

Today, I proudly help buyers and sellers throughout San Diego County, specializing in military relocation, VA loans, and helping Veterans make the most of their hard-earned benefits.

Because to me, real estate isn't just about buying or selling a house. It's about helping families build their next chapter, one home at a time.

Relationships over transactions. Always.
